About

I am a Senior Data Scientist at the Institute of Smart Systems and Artificial Intelligence (ISSAI) at Nazarbayev University, where I have worked since 2023. I have an M.S. and a B.S. in Computer Science from Nazarbayev University.

Most of my work so far is on speech recognition for Kazakh and Russian. My recent work is on LLM agents: agents that adapt a speech recognition model to a new speech domain, and rule-based controllers built from the agents' traces that run without an LLM. I would like to continue working on LLM agents.

Interests: LLM agents · fine-tuning of language and speech models · low-resource languages

Current work

  • LLM agents that adapt a speech recognition model under review

    LLM agents adapt a speech recognition model (Whisper) to a new speech domain, for example telephone, accented or medical speech, without losing quality on other speech. Across 27 runs with three LLMs, the agents improved the word error rate on held-out data in every domain. From the agents' traces I built rule-based controllers that run without an LLM.

    Manuscript, 2026

  • AI clinical scribe for Kazakh and Russian ongoing

    A collaboration with Prof. Helen Chen's group at the University of Waterloo on an AI clinical scribe for Kazakh and Russian doctor–patient conversations. I contribute the speech recognition side.

Publications

  • Persona-ASR: Bilingual Target-Speaker Speech Recognition for Kazakh–English Overlapping Speech

    Meiramov, R.† (equal contribution), Rakhimzhanova, T.† (equal contribution), Taibassarov, A., Makhataeva, Z., and Varol, H. A.

    Machine Learning and Knowledge Extraction, 8(8), 246 · 2026 · DOI for Persona-ASR · Code for Persona-ASR
    † Equal contribution

    Target-speaker ASR transcribes one chosen speaker from overlapping speech. Persona-ASR is the first such system for Kazakh. It adds an explicit step that decides whether the target speaker is present, which turns silent hallucination for an absent speaker into a decision that can be evaluated. The paper also releases two bilingual Kazakh–English benchmarks, KazMix3 and PersonaMix.

  • Improved Kazakh Named Entity Transcription Using Synthetic Speech

    Meiramov, R. and Varol, H. A.

    UBMK, 10th International Conference on Computer Science and Engineering (IEEE), Istanbul, Türkiye · 2025 · DOI for the named entity paper · Data and code for the named entity paper

    Synthetic speech with LLM-based entity substitution improved the transcription of Kazakh named entities. The paper introduces a named-entity WER metric and a human-read test set, to verify that the gains held on real speech.

Models and code

  • Whisper for Kazakh and Russian

    Fine-tuned OpenAI Whisper for Kazakh and Russian. Released whisper-large-v3-turbo-kk-lora, a Kazakh LoRA adapter trained on a single 16 GB consumer GPU, with training and evaluation code and cross-domain results on three test sets.

  • Fine-tuning LLMs: Kazakh questions to SQL

    Materials for a two-day course on fine-tuning LLMs that I taught to ML engineers in October 2026. One small model (Qwen3-1.7B) learns to turn questions in Kazakh into SQL queries. The steps are SFT with QLoRA, DPO, GRPO, evaluation and serving with vLLM.

    Code for the course · Dataset · Models: SFT, DPO, GRPO
